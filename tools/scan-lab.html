Scan Lab

Reads flatbed / sheet-fed card scans and reports the same foil numbers the phone measures, so scanner readings can be compared directly against the torch-lab fixtures. Everything runs locally — nothing uploads. Opened off disk it's a drag-and-drop inspector; started via node tools/scan-lab-server.mjs it becomes an in/out workflow: scans dropped in the in/ folder are read, classified and filed into per-family folders, and tagging the true rarity refiles the scan under confirmed/ as a human-trusted label.

Standalone mode — no server detected. Drag-and-drop works, but nothing is watched or filed into folders. For the in/out workflow run node tools/scan-lab-server.mjs and open the http://127.0.0.1:… address it prints (not this file:// page).

Readings

Every card is reading specular 0. Even the scanner profile's low bars aren't being crossed — the scans may be very dark, or the card area is wrong. Check the region boxes on the previews before trusting any verdict here.

Tag the true rarity on each card before exporting — the numbers are only useful next to a known-correct label. Check the region boxes drawn on the preview actually sit on the name plate and the art; if auto-detect got the card edges wrong, drag a box on the preview to set the card area by hand.

What the numbers mean

specular — fraction of pixels bright enough to read as a glint (luminance ≥ 0.8). hueSpread — circular spread of hues among bright, saturated pixels, 0 = one colour, 1 = all over the wheel; this is what separates rainbow foils. goldness — fraction of bright pixels in the gold hue band (38–68°). Region boxes and sampling mirror readFoilStats / regionStat in mobile/src/services/scanner.ts; if that changes, change this too or the readings stop being comparable.

Two verdicts are computed. The pill (and the filing) uses the scanner profile — much lower glint bars, because a flatbed holds exposure below clipping and foil never blows out the way it does on a phone. The phone-threshold read shown under the reasons applies the app's exact thresholds to the same numbers, for comparing against phone fixtures. Scanner thresholds are provisional and get retuned from the tagged readings accumulating in the manifest. A flat scan also can't see embossing or angle-shift — Ultimate Rares read as their glint pattern.
