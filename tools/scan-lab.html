Scan Lab

Reads flatbed / sheet-fed card scans and reports the same foil numbers the phone measures, so scanner readings can be compared directly against the torch-lab fixtures. Everything runs locally in this page — nothing uploads.

Drop card scans here — or click to pick files
PNG, BMP, JPEG. TIFF needs an internet connection the first time.

Readings

Every card is reading specular 0. That is the expected result if the scanner is holding exposure below clipping — the phone's specular counts pixels at luminance ≥ 0.8, a bar tuned for camera glare that a well-exposed scan may never cross. It does not mean the scans are unusable: hueSpread and goldness are still measured over whatever bright pixels exist, and if those separate your rarities the threshold is what needs rethinking, not the scan.

Tag the true rarity on each card before exporting — the numbers are only useful next to a known-correct label. Check the region boxes drawn on the preview actually sit on the name plate and the art; if auto-detect got the card edges wrong, drag a box on the preview to set the card area by hand.

What the numbers mean

specular — fraction of pixels bright enough to read as a glint (luminance ≥ 0.8). hueSpread — circular spread of hues among bright, saturated pixels, 0 = one colour, 1 = all over the wheel; this is what separates rainbow foils. goldness — fraction of bright pixels in the gold hue band (38–68°). Region boxes, sampling and thresholds mirror readFoilStats / regionStat in mobile/src/services/scanner.ts; if that changes, change this too or the readings stop being comparable.
